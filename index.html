Got Questions?

Everything You Need to Know

Is this legitimate? How do I know it's not a scam?
FlexEarn SG is a registered Singapore business. We have paid out over $2.1 million to more than 8,400 members this year. We will never ask you for upfront fees, never involve you in any MLM or pyramid scheme, and never ask you to transfer money. You can verify our UEN on the ACRA BizFile portal.
Do I need any qualifications or experience?
No. We take on members from all backgrounds — fresh grads, retirees, housewives, and everyone in between. All training is provided. If you can follow instructions and communicate clearly, you qualify.
How and when do I get paid?
Payouts are processed weekly every Friday and transferred directly to your Singapore bank account (PAYNOW or bank transfer). Your first payout typically arrives within 7 days of your first completed task.
What is the minimum number of hours I must commit?
There is no minimum. This is genuinely flexible — start with 5 hours a week, ramp up to 25, or take a week off. You decide what works for your schedule. We only ask that you communicate your availability clearly.
Can I do this alongside my full-time job?
Absolutely. Most of our members keep their day jobs. FlexEarn is designed specifically as a supplementary income stream. Many members do their FlexEarn tasks in the evenings, over lunch, or on weekends.
Is there a registration fee to join?
No. Joining FlexEarn is completely free. If any platform or person ever asks you to pay to join, that is not us — please report it. You earn money here. You never spend it to join.
