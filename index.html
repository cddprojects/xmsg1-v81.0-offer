FlexEarn matches members to service and fulfilment tasks based on your background and availability. All work is done remotely — no physical presence required. Full onboarding and task guidance is provided. We'll explain exactly what's involved when we speak with you.

Payouts are processed every Friday and sent directly to your PayNow-linked account or bank account via transfer. Your first payout typically arrives within 7 days of your first completed task. There are no minimum thresholds, no holding periods.

No minimum hours. Start with 5 hours a week, ramp up to 25, or take a week off when life requires it. We ask only that you communicate your availability clearly so we can match you to tasks appropriately.
