Is this legitimate? How do I know it's not a scam?
That's the right question to ask, and we respect you for asking it. FlexEarn SG is a registered Singapore business — you can verify our UEN on the ACRA BizFile portal. We have paid out over $2.1 million to more than 8,400 members. We will never ask you for upfront fees, never involve you in MLM, and never ask you to transfer money to anyone. If something ever feels off, call us directly — our team is based here in Singapore.
Do I need experience or qualifications?
No. We work with people from all walks of life — fresh graduates, retirees, homemakers, career changers. All training is provided. If you can follow clear instructions and communicate honestly, you're qualified. We care about reliability and attitude, not your resume.
How and when do I get paid?
Payouts are processed every Friday and sent directly to your Singapore bank account via PayNow or direct transfer. Your very first payout typically arrives within 7 days of completing your first task. There are no holding periods, no thresholds to hit, no minimum earnings before you can withdraw.
Is there a minimum number of hours I have to commit?
No. There is genuinely no minimum. Start with 5 hours a week. Take a week off if you need to. Ramp up when life allows. We only ask that you communicate with us clearly — if you're not available, just let us know. We'll work around it.
Can I do this while keeping my full-time job?
That's exactly what most of our members do. FlexEarn was designed as a secondary income stream. Most people do their tasks in the evenings, during lunch, or on weekends. It fits around a full-time job — we've built it that way intentionally.
Is there any fee to register?
No. Never. It will always be completely free to join FlexEarn. If anyone ever tells you there's a registration fee or a starter kit to purchase — that is not us. Please report it. You come here to earn money, not to spend it.
